# Review Pack: VA Single-Family Overlay

Pack id: `pack-va-single-family-overlay-v1`

The VA overlay is separate from FHA. Rules are not shared unless explicitly listed in the VA pack.

## Initial Checks

- VA case or assignment identifier where extractable
- VA exhibit presence
- MPR-related language routed as reviewer questions
- Repair observation and conclusion consistency
- Order-specific VA instructions

## Safety Limits

CAS does not declare MPR failure, invent Tidewater requirements, or expose internal VA reviewer analysis to clients unless an authorized reviewer approves visibility.
